Yaesu FT-450 — CW Beacon Memory Aide-Mémoire

Companion sheet to the Yaesu FT-450 / FT-450D operating manual for programming and sending CW Beacon text banks. Assumes normal HF station operation is already understood.

1. What this feature does

The FT-450 stores up to three CW message banks (BCN TXT 1, 2, and 3). Each bank holds up to 40 characters. Banks can be chained for longer text (up to 118 characters total), or kept separate and played one at a time.

For a Silent Key / Final Call style ceremony, keep the banks separate:

Bank Typical use Played with
1 Final Call (three calls of the SK callsign) PLAY1
2 Closing (NIL / 73 / SK) PLAY2
3 Unused, or a third message PLAY3

2. Example messages (VE1NB Last Call from VE0CNM)

Substitute callsigns for the next event. Keep each bank under 40 characters.

Bank Example text Count
1 VE1NB VE1NB VE1NB DE VE0CNM K 36
2 VE1NB DE VE0CNM NIL 73 SK 27

Generic pattern:

SKCALL SKCALL SKCALL DE YOURCALL K
SKCALL DE YOURCALL NIL 73 SK

3. Two display quirks that matter

Arrow → means “continue to the next bank.”

On the radio LCD this special character looks like an arrow →. Some printed manuals show it as “Æ”. It is not a quiet end-of-message mark. If → is placed after Bank 1 text, PLAY1 will send Bank 1 and then Bank 2 immediately.

For separate one-shot messages: end each bank on the last real letter only. Leave unused slots as spaces. Do not put → at the end.

BCN TIM = OFF stops timed repeating of a finished beacon cycle. It does not stop bank chaining. Chaining is controlled only by →.

Space character and “ghost” slots.

Space on this LCD often looks like a tiny “u” or small blank glyph. Find it by turning the large dial clockwise through the character list to the end, then select that space glyph. Confirm with a play test: a true space is a pause; a wrong glyph sends Morse.

If Bank 1 needs many turns of the large dial before letters appear, leftover (“ghost”) characters are still in that bank. Overwrite every slot in the bank with spaces before entering new text. Bank 2 and 3 often look clean simply because they were empty. A factory reset clears this fully if needed.

4. Controls used for beacon programming

Control Use in this procedure
[F] Hold 1 second to enter or leave Menu mode
[DSP/SEL] Turn = select menu item or move character cursor / Push = enter or save
[MAIN DIAL] Select characters while editing BCN TXT
MODE Set CW before sending
[C.S/VOICE] Start or stop the assigned PLAY memory

5. Enable the extended menu

  1. Hold [F] one second to enter Menu mode.
  2. Turn [DSP/SEL] to EXT MNU.
  3. Press [DSP/SEL].
  4. Turn [DSP/SEL] to ON.
  5. Press [DSP/SEL].
  6. Hold [F] one second to exit.

6. Clear the three beacon banks

Clear before every new event so ghost characters cannot corrupt spaces or chaining.

  1. Hold [F] one second. Select BCN TXT. Press [DSP/SEL].
  2. Turn [MAIN DIAL] to bank 1:.
  3. Turn [DSP/SEL] one click clockwise to edit.
  4. For every character slot in the bank: set a space with [MAIN DIAL], then advance with [DSP/SEL] clockwise.
  5. Return to bank selection. Repeat for banks 2: and 3:.
  6. Hold [F] one second to save and exit.

7. Program a bank

  1. Hold [F] one second. Select BCN TXT. Press [DSP/SEL].
  2. Turn [MAIN DIAL] to the desired bank (1:, 2:, or 3:).
  3. Turn [DSP/SEL] one click clockwise to edit.
  4. For each character of the message:
    1. Turn [MAIN DIAL] to the letter, number, or space.
    2. Turn [DSP/SEL] one click clockwise to advance.
  5. Stop on the last real letter of the message.
  6. Confirm the next slot is a space, not →.
  7. Hold [F] one second to save and exit.

Example — Bank 1 entry order:

V E 1 N B [space] V E 1 N B [space] V E 1 N B [space] D E [space] V E 0 C N M [space] K

Example — Bank 2 entry order:

V E 1 N B [space] D E [space] V E 0 C N M [space] N I L [space] 7 3 [space] S K

8. Set CW speed

  1. Hold [F] one second. Select CWSPEED. Press [DSP/SEL].
  2. Turn [DSP/SEL] to the desired speed in WPM.
  3. If the display shows CPM, press [F] once to switch to WPM, then set the speed.
  4. Press [DSP/SEL] to lock.
  5. Hold [F] one second to exit.

Suggested Silent Key / Final Call speed: 12 WPM.

9. Set one-shot play (no timed repeat)

  1. Hold [F] one second. Select BCN TIM. Press [DSP/SEL].
  2. Turn [DSP/SEL] to OFF.
  3. Press [DSP/SEL].
  4. Hold [F] one second to exit.

10. Assign PLAY1 / PLAY2 / PLAY3 to the front button

Only one PLAY assignment is active on [C.S/VOICE] at a time. To send Bank 1, assign PLAY1. To send Bank 2 later, change the assignment to PLAY2.

  1. Hold [F] one second. Select PNL-C.S. Press [DSP/SEL].
  2. Turn [DSP/SEL] to PLAY1, PLAY2, or PLAY3.
  3. Press [DSP/SEL].
  4. Hold [F] one second to exit.

11. Send a bank

  1. Set MODE to CW.
  2. Confirm PNL-C.S is set to the desired PLAY bank.
  3. Press [C.S/VOICE] once to start.
  4. Press [C.S/VOICE] again to stop if needed.

Expected result with no → at the end of the bank: the selected bank plays once and stops.

12. If Bank 1 runs straight into Bank 2

  1. Open BCN TXT for bank 1:.
  2. Step to the character after the last letter of the message.
  3. Change → to a space.
  4. Repeat for bank 2: if → is present after its last letter.
  5. Save, set PNL-C.S = PLAY1, mode CW, and play again. Bank 1 must stop on its own.

13. Suggested Silent Key on-air order

  1. Voice Final Call on SSB (three calls; nothing heard; 73).
  2. Switch to CW. Confirm PNL-C.S = PLAY1.
  3. Press [C.S/VOICE] — Bank 1 Final Call.
  4. Observe silence after Bank 1 finishes.
  5. Change PNL-C.S to PLAY2.
  6. Press [C.S/VOICE] — Bank 2 closing (NIL 73 SK).

Example voice outline:

This is [YOURCALL], [station name if used].

Calling [name], [SKCALL].

[SKCALL], this is [YOURCALL].

[pause]

[SKCALL], [SKCALL], this is [YOURCALL].

[pause]

[SKCALL], [SKCALL], [SKCALL], this is [YOURCALL].

[pause]

[Name], [SKCALL], is acknowledged as a Silent Key.

From [YOURCALL]: 73.

14. Quick checklist

  • EXT MNU = ON
  • Banks 1–3 cleared to spaces (ghost characters removed)
  • Bank texts entered; no → after the last letter of each bank
  • CWSPEED set (12 WPM typical for Final Call)
  • BCN TIM = OFF
  • PLAY1 tested: plays and stops
  • PLAY2 tested: plays and stops
  • PNL-C.S left on PLAY1 if Bank 1 is the first CW send
